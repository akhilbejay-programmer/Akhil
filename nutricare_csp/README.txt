NutriCare - Community Service Project Web App
==============================================
Includes:
- index.html (Main website featuring the 8-Week Timeline at the bottom of the page)
- style.css (Liquid Glass UI styling and custom scrollbars)
- script.js (Interactive modals, lightbox image previews, and Lucide initialization)
- images/ (Directory where your field photos week1.jpg through week8.jpeg and member1-4 belong)
