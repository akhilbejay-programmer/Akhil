8-Week Community Journey

Week-by-week field engagement, nutrition surveys, and school awareness drives conducted in Nellore.

Community House Visits

Conducted doorstep family surveys to evaluate local household dietary staples and routines.

WEEK 03

Awareness & BMI Camp

Presented visual food pyramids and calculated biometric BMI benchmarks at KNM Elementary School.

High School Delegation

Coordinated campaign modules with the Headmistress of KNR Municipal Corporation High School.

WEEK 05

Fruit Distribution & Hygiene

Shared sanitation hygiene routines and distributed seasonal fruit rations to school children.

WEEK 06

Classroom Engagement

Held interactive Q&A workshops breaking down essential vitamins, hydration, and avoiding processed foods.

Web & Data Synthesis

Final Academic Submission

Submitted the comprehensive 8-week CSP portfolio and community metrics to the department reviewers.

THE NUTRITION REVOLUTION

"Good Nutrition for Good Health Creating Awareness About Healthy Food and Balanced Diet Among Rural Communities A Healthier Community Begins with a Healthier Plate! Our Community Service Project in Nellore, Andhra Pradesh, is dedicated to building a healthier future by educating rural families about the importance of balanced nutrition, healthy eating habits, and safe drinking water. Through awareness campaigns and community engagement, we encourage families to embrace nutritious local foods, fresh fruits, green vegetables, pulses, whole grains, and protein-rich meals while reducing their dependence on junk food and highly processed packaged products".

Carried out in Nellore, Andhra Pradesh, this community service project empowers families to substitute nutrient-poor packaged foods with balanced local staples, pulses, vegetables, and sanitary drinking water.
