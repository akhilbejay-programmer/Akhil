HEALTH AWARENESS

Small Habits. Better Health.

Simple health-awareness messages for our Community Service Project.

🥦

Eat a Variety

Include a variety of vegetables, fruits, grains, legumes and suitable protein sources as part of a balanced diet.

🍎

Eat More Fruits & Vegetables

Include different fruits and vegetables as part of regular balanced meals.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink suitable amounts of fluids regularly and pay attention to hydration during everyday activities.

🏃

Keep Moving

Include regular physical activity such as walking and other suitable activities in your daily routine.

🧂

Limit Excess Salt

Avoid excessive salt intake and become familiar with the sodium content of packaged foods.

🍬

Limit Free Sugars

Reduce frequent consumption of foods and drinks that contain high amounts of free sugars.

🧼

Maintain Food Hygiene

Wash hands, keep utensils clean and follow safe food handling and storage practices.

🌾

Choose Whole Foods

Where practical, choose a variety of minimally processed foods as part of a balanced eating pattern.

😴

Maintain Healthy Routines

A healthy lifestyle involves several habits including nutrition, activity, rest and personal hygiene.

CSP team Note: This website provides general health and nutrition awareness information for an educational project. It is not a medical diagnosis or treatment system.
